## Findings

**F1 [Critical, §6, Completeness/Generality]** — The frozen affine family `y=a+b·u` with **both** parameters free defends only against wrong *pairing* of anchors to canonicals; it has no defense against a wholly fabricated but self-consistent claim. With exactly 2 free parameters, any n≥3 points a claimant *declares* to be affine in u fit with rms≈0 by construction — the claimant doesn't need real physics, just two invented numbers (a,b) applied to the harness-detected anchors. The T1–T4 probe only tested the *identity-shift* attack (values are the true ones, misassigned to neighbors); it never tested a *value-fabrication* attack (declare any (a,b), e.g. a=0,b=1, entirely untethered to physics). This is the multiplicative/intercept analogue of the intercept-absorption problem §8.2a already flags for `a` — but nothing constrains `b`. This is the same "statistic computed from the thing it audits cannot contradict it" pattern already catalogued (`docs/*downstream-of-decision*`): the fit is derived *from* the claim and then used to validate that same claim. CLAUDE.md's own justifying claim ("an over-determined fit... is checkable with no reference table") is true only for the *pairing* problem, not the *value* problem, and the spec doesn't disclose this boundary. **Fix**: either (a) pin one parameter to a harness-external, domain-general constant (e.g., treat the family as verification of a *pre-committed* (a,b) rather than a fit-then-check of a claimant-supplied one — this is also the harness-as-commitment-device idea §7 already argues for, just not applied here), or (b) explicitly scope §6's guarantee to "defeats misassignment among honestly-derived values" and add a companion mechanism/probe for value-fabrication before claiming the merge "is the check."

**F2 [Critical, §6, Completeness]** — Nothing requires a claim to address **all** harness-detected anchors. D3 measured n=17 real peaks; §6 only requires n≥3. A claimant free to *select which 3 (or more) of the 17* to submit landings for can cherry-pick a subset that happens to lie near a fabricated line while silently ignoring the 14 that would contradict it — reintroducing exactly the claimant-controlled degrees of freedom the redesign exists to remove (cf. memory: "canonical + delta both claimant-supplied... fix = harness enumerates the family and emits a survivor set"). §6.1 says the survivor set is "never trimmed by expected count" on the *divide* side, but says nothing about the *merge* side requiring the full survivor set (or a harness-chosen, not claimant-chosen, subset). **Fix**: require the claim to cover the full detected anchor set, or have the harness (not the claimant) select which subset is graded.

**F3 [Important, §6, Claim-Evidence-Trace]** — §6's "Falsifiable expectation" — "raman's minimal-thrash class is plausibly caused by the shipped gate's inability to accept a correct answer (F4: right physics, inexpressible)" — cites F4 by name as live support, but the spec's own §10 provenance list cites `f4-retraction-20260820/retraction.md`, which retracts exactly this inference: the real oracle (`1e7/x`) was **already expressible under plain `reciprocal`, already in the whitelist**; the models' "inexpressible" 2-op derivation was a fabrication anchored on baseline (not peak) x-values, not a case of correct physics the gate couldn't accept. Citing "F4: right physics, inexpressible" without noting the retraction risks smuggling a refuted causal story into a redesign's success metric — precisely the kind of downstream-of-decision confirmation this project has already burned cycles on. **Fix**: either drop the F4 parenthetical, or replace it with the narrower surviving claim ("the whitelist cannot express a genuine two-free-parameter relationship," per retraction's "What survives of F4") and register the raman-thrash-drop prediction against *that*, not the retracted inference.

**F4 [Important, §4 G1, Claim-Evidence-Trace]** — G1 cites "F3/O4: announcing a check moves the failure" as a **measured, mandatory** guard basis. But `f3-cell-contract-20260820/verdict.md` never scores or reports the O4 arm at all — its "Spend: 8 sonnet calls (O2×4, O3×4)" line silently excludes O4's 4 calls, despite `pre-registration.md` explicitly registering O4 with its own decision rule ("4/4 → adopt... ≤2/4 → confirms prediction"). I read the raw `out-O4-r{1..4}.json` cells directly: `strictBlock` is `malformed, malformed, none, malformed` — i.e. **0/4**, below even the registered ≤2/4 failure bar, and consistent with the pre-registered prediction (root cause is F4, not F3). The underlying data likely *does* support G1's point, but it was never formally verdicted — an unclosed loop being cited as settled in a design doc, against this project's own pre-registration discipline. **Fix**: score O4 formally in the f3-cell-contract verdict (cheap — the cells already exist), then cite that verdict; or have dnc-design.md cite the raw cells directly with its own stated reasoning instead of an implied-settled "F3/O4" shorthand.

**F5 [Important, §4 G3 / §7, Claim-Evidence-Trace]** — G3's "prose lesson-following measured 1/8" traces to `docs/reboot.md:150` ("advisory prose gripped 1/8"), which is **not** in §10's provenance list for this spec. Separately, §7's "4 measured reasons" (no commitment device in a single context; playbook never asks; timeout economics punish probing; instruction alone under-actuates) — only the last is traceable to a cited verdict (reval-adherence F5's fabrication finding); the other three are asserted with "measured" framing but I could not find them measured anywhere in the given evidentiary base or via search of `docs/`. The correct number (1/8) is real, but the citation trail for both instances is broken relative to what the spec claims to argue from. **Fix**: add the missing sources to §10, or downgrade "4 measured reasons" to distinguish measured-here from carried-over-from-elsewhere.

**F6 [Important, §5 vs §6, Internal Coherence]** — §5 point 3 rules, based on addendum-01 A2, that any future classification pass must "lead with the within-task fail/pass ratio... raw distributions reported, never thresholded booleans (the probe's own M6 minimum manufactured the class boundary it counted)." §6's own falsifiable expectation then predicts "raman minimal-thrash count should DROP" — reusing the exact thresholded-boolean metric §5 just ruled unfit for future measurement. The spec should apply its own methodological correction to its own next measurement: register the raw repeat-count distribution shift, not the M6-thresholded class-drop.

**F7 [Important, §3, Internal Coherence]** — §3(a)'s load-bearing clause states an axis is invalid if its merge "needs the answer, reintroducing claimant degrees of freedom at merge time (the revalidator-bypass defect, at the merge)," and Raman is explicitly routed to §6 as the principled alternative to a merge that needs the answer. Applying §3(a)'s own bar to §6 itself (per F1/F2 above), §6 has narrowed the claimant's degrees of freedom (from "any landing anywhere in-range" to "an affine claim over harness-fixed anchors, not necessarily all of them, both parameters free") but has not eliminated them. Either soften §3(a)'s framing of §6 as "solved," or fold F1/F2's fix into §6 before treating it as satisfying §3(a).

**F8 [Important, §4, Implementability]** — Of the three registered L1 bullets, only bullet 2 ("When a long input has produced errors...") matches the review-gate's required phrasing template (`propose.ts:1611`: "a trigger... or a hard-gate... no other phrasing passes review"). Bullets 1 and 3 are neither form. Separately, the gate's SCOPE REQUIREMENT (`propose.ts:1487-1498`) requires every bullet's trigger to name an observable run-state condition "tied to the failure class it targets **in the measured taxonomy above**" — i.e., bullets are meant to be generated from a specific ab-run's diagnosed taxonomy, not authored a priori and injected as fixed "registered wording." There is no existing command that takes hand-written bullet text and stages it as a candidate through the standard review gate; `createCandidate()` is generic enough to be scripted around it, but doing so means these three bullets would bypass the phrasing/SCOPE checks every other candidate bullet in this harness goes through. **Fix**: either rewrite bullets 1 and 3 to the trigger/hard-gate templates and route them through the standard diagnosis-driven proposer (accepting the model may rephrase them, weakening the "registered wording" claim), or specify explicitly that L1's injection path is a new, harness-machinery script that deliberately bypasses SCOPE (and say why that's acceptable here).

**F9 [Important, §6, Implementability]** — The peak detector (§6.1/D3) needs the **full raw numeric series** (1500 rows) to sweep windows 5..101; the existing `buildSample`/`summarizeFile` pipeline that feeds the audit model deliberately truncates to head/tail-20 lines + a min/max range (by design, to bound `DEFAULT_BUDGET_BYTES`). None of the current `convention-audit.ts` data flow carries a full series anywhere. `probe.py` sidesteps this by reading the fixture file directly by a hardcoded path. A real implementation needs either (a) the merge/divide step to run harness-side against the raw task fixture (reusing `parseCopySources`-style leak-safe resolution, generalized to locate "the numeric data file(s)" per task) entirely separate from the audit sample, or (b) a redesigned sample format that carries the full series. The spec doesn't say which, and this is a real ambiguity a builder hits immediately (properly deferred per §9's non-goals, but worth flagging now since it affects whether §6's design as stated is even wire-compatible with anything in the codebase).

**F10 [Minor, §6, Generality]** — Frozen family `{x, 1/x}` justified as "general measurement algebra," defensible in the abstract (wavelength/wavenumber, period/frequency dualities are genuinely domain-general). But its concrete membership was chosen the same day as, and downstream of, F4's chain revealing raman's true relationship is exactly `u=1/x` — a timing/hindsight risk CLAUDE.md explicitly warns about ("declared and version-controlled does not make an answer key legitimate"). §8.3 correctly commits to an oracle-set-and-bad-set validation for the frozen family before this is load-bearing; recommend the bad set specifically include a fixture whose true relationship is provably outside `{x,1/x}` (e.g. quadratic/log) to test that residuals reject it, not merely that {x,1/x} "fits" raman.

**F11 [Minor, §8.2, Decision Hygiene]** — The pre-committed R-threshold acceptance rule ("the fixed threshold survives only if the noisy gap stays ≥2 orders of magnitude across the sweep") is good practice (correctly identifies the fitted-constant risk before any verdict exists) but under-specified relative to T1-T4's rigor: no registered noise distribution, magnitude range, trial count, or whether "the gap" means worst-case or average across the sweep. As written it's vague enough to be reinterpreted at verdict time exactly the way the rule was designed to prevent. Recommend pre-registering these parameters with the same specificity as the merge-fit probe's test matrix before spending on it.

**F12 [Minor, §8.3, Decision Hygiene]** — §8.2a's claim that "every future family member... inherits the conditioning-check requirement automatically" has no enforcement mechanism (no lint rule, no required regression test tied to family-member addition) — it's a documentation promise, not a checked one. Recommend a concrete gate (e.g., a test asserting every family member with nonzero intercept-freedom has an associated T1-style regression case).

**F13 [Minor, §6/§4, Completeness]** — The conditioning check as measured/registered only tests ±1-index-shift alternates (`fit_affine(us[:-1], cs[1:])` and the mirror). Other degeneracy shapes — reversal, ±2 shift, a 2-element swap, or symmetric constellations with multiple near-equal-quality wrong pairings — are neither tested nor argued to reduce to the ±1 case. §6.4 states the check is "REQUIRED, measured necessary" but that's proven only for the one attack class actually built (per CLAUDE.md's own method: "build the input that should break it" — this input wasn't built for the wider permutation space). Recommend extending the regression set before this is treated as closed.

**F14 [Observation, §3, Claim-Evidence-Trace]** — §3's worked examples ("gcode's current fixture has a low-coupling glyph axis," "Raman is REFUSED... routed to §6") are stated as settled fact but aren't tied to a citation in §10's provenance list; addendum-02 only validates the *criterion* on "ONE fixture" (lane-B, unnamed in the given evidentiary base) without an explicit gcode-application verdict. Not a hard problem since §8.1 already flags the second fixture as the standing transfer test, but the review couldn't independently verify the gcode/raman applications as "measured" rather than "illustrated."

**F15 [Observation, §6, Decision Hygiene]** — Positive note: the fail-closed semantics (§6.4, "degenerate constellations are UNCHECKABLE, not wrong; no inject, never a pass") are unambiguous, match T2's measured behavior exactly, and are consistently restated across §6 and the verdict — this part of the design is binding as written, no gaming surface found.

## Overall verdict

**SOUND-WITH-AMENDMENTS**

The empirical backbone (T1–T4/D1–D4 merge-fit results, the bimodal length-vs-difficulty classification, the axis-selection rank-vs-select distinction) is unusually well-earned — numbers trace cleanly to their cited verdicts, and the spec is honest about what remains unmeasured (§8, "Not measured" sections). The document correctly defers arming/implementation to future increments (§9), which is the right posture given the gaps found here.

However, §6 — the spec's central architectural claim ("the merge is the check") — has two structural gaps (F1: no defense against wholesale value fabrication since both affine parameters are free; F2: no requirement to address the full anchor set, reopening cherry-picking) that mean the design, as currently specified, has not actually closed the "claimant degrees of freedom at merge time" problem it exists to solve — it has narrowed the attack surface measured (identity-shift) without addressing the attack surface not yet built (value-shift, subset-shift). These must be resolved — not just implemented — before §6 is treated as validated. Additionally, citation hygiene is uneven in three places (F3, F4, F5): claims labeled "measured" lean on a retracted finding, an unscored experimental arm, and an uncited external source respectively. None of these are fatal to the document's direction, but all should be fixed before this spec is used to authorize the next-increment build gos it explicitly defers to.
