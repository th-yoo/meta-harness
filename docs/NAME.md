# kkamak (까막)

**Project name, sealed 2026-07-25.** Pronounced *"kkah-mahk"*. From Korean
까마귀 (crow) / 까막까치 (crows-and-magpies) / 까막.

## Why this name

1. **까막까치 — the Ojakgyo bridge (오작교).** In the Gyeonwu-and-Jiknyeo legend,
   crows and magpies — plain, unglamorous birds — assemble themselves once a
   year into the bridge that lets the separated lovers cross the Milky Way.
   This project is that bridge: borrowed, ordinary components (AHE's method,
   Stop-hook and MutGen patterns, curated prompts, classical statistics)
   composed into a crossing none of them makes alone. Not one bird in stolen
   feathers — many birds, together, being a bridge.
2. **까마귀 날자 배 떨어진다 — "the crow flies, the pear falls."** The Korean
   proverb about spurious causation: blaming the crow for a coincidence. The
   exact fallacy this project's machinery exists to kill — every Fisher test,
   every one-variable arm, every provenance refusal, every attribution
   correction in the ledger is a refusal to convict the crow because a pear
   fell. The name carries the project's statistical conscience as a proverb.
3. **까막눈 — "crow-eyed."** Unable to truly read: a humble echo for a system
   built on a model whose understanding must be PROVEN through gates, never
   trusted on its word.

## How it was found

The naming question began with an originality audit (2026-07-25, resume.md
ledger): most of our components are borrowed by design. That led to Aesop's
vain jackdaw — the bird in borrowed plumes, exposed when the flock reclaims
its feathers. Apt, with one inversion that is our originality: this project
runs the plucking itself (the adoption gate, guards, held-out arms — the
flock attacking the plumage on schedule; v8, v9, v10 = feathers reclaimed,
publicly, in the ledger). We went looking for the borrowing bird and came
home with the bridge-building ones.

Candidates considered:
- **Jackdaw** — rejected on collisions: two established Kafka libraries
  (FundingCircle, obsidiandynamics), a known AD-recon infosec tool (PyPI),
  Shell's Jackdaw gas field dominating search, and an AI-sessions product
  (jackdaw.whattheflock.dev) squatting our exact category.
- **Graculus** (Latin jackdaw, Phaedrus' *Graculus superbus et pavo*) —
  collision-clean (only a Yorkshire copper-bird sculptor and a dormant UK
  ltd) but a fetched Latin feather; kept here as the trail marker.
- **kkamak** — chosen: collision-clean in software (only a Korean game
  streamer's handle and unrelated differently-spelled companies), short,
  CLI-fine, and the owner's own coinage in the owner's own language, with
  two Korean crow-proverbs that map onto the project better than the fable
  that started the search.

A coincidence recorded for the record: the naming session ran on a model
named Fable — the fable model, mid-fable, naming the crow project.
